# Code of Conduct

Remote Host MCP welcomes respectful technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate clearly and professionally;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- provide reproducible evidence for technical claims when practical;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing private operational data;
- accept maintainers' decisions on safety boundaries, scope, and release quality even when proposing alternatives;
- help keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted abuse, deliberate disruption, publication of another person's private information, or knowingly posting unsafe operational material after a maintainer asks for removal are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or project operations.

Security-sensitive reports should use the process in `SECURITY.md` rather than ordinary public discussion.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled collaboration spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and release/support interactions.
